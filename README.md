Key Findings & Insights
Average Dimensions: Virginica has the highest average petal length, while Setosa is significantly shorter and more compact.

Data Distribution & Outliers: Setosa shows several outliers both above and below its median. Versicolor also has one lower outlier, whereas Virginica exhibits a clean distribution with no extreme values.

Species Separability: Setosa is completely isolated from the other species in terms of petal dimensions, making it the most distinct and easiest category to classify.
